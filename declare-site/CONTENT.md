## Problem
In America about 60% of high schools offer computer science or AI courses.
In Nepal almost none of us get taught how AI works at all.
Most students hear about AI constantly and never learn what is behind it.
That gap is what we work on.

Sources: Code.org State of AI + CS 2025, AISCliteracy arXiv:2506.23321

## What we teach
01 The smallest algorithms
   We start with linear regression, small enough to work through by hand.
02 How a network learns
   Weights, error, and the loop that makes a model improve.
03 Attention
   The mechanism running inside every chatbot students already use.
04 How to explain it
   Public speaking, so students can teach what they learned to other people.

## How it works
1. You get in touch
   Message us with your school name and roughly how many students.
2. We plan the days
   Usually three sessions. We work around your timetable.
3. We run it
   We bring everything. The school provides a room and students.
4. Students keep practising
   Everyone gets access to our speech practice tool afterwards.

## Practice tool
Students get a free speech coach they can keep using after we leave.
Record a speech, get feedback on structure, pace and clarity, and track progress.
