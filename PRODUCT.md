# Aawaz Speaker Coach

A browser-based AI public speaking coach for students practising debates, MUN, school speeches and college presentations. The homepage introduces the coach and leads to `/coach`.

The current public copy describes a free first report without an account, daily limits, and an optional account for history. The coach evaluates five speech formats, with feedback on structure, filler words, pace and pauses. Existing data-handling copy and route metadata remain authoritative.

## Confirmed design constraints

Preserve the purple and lavender palette, the report UI cards, the animated phone report, and Aawax. Retain separate navigation pills and alternating light and dark sections. Improve composition and mobile readability. Use no small eyebrow text above headings, no em dashes in visible copy, and no unrelated decorative gradients. Keep native scrolling and respect reduced motion.

## Scope

These instructions describe the public homepage and shared marketing frame. They do not authorize a redesign of the coach application, changes to scoring, or changes to privacy and account behavior.

The current visual direction includes custom rehearsal imagery, liquid glass controls adapted from React Bits, and a working speech-opening comparison. Generated images are editorial artwork, not customer evidence.
